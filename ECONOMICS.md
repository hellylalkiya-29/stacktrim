# Financial Engineering & Monetization Architecture

## 1. Enterprise Unit Economics
StackTrim.ai operates on an automated value-share operational framework. Rather than forcing standard friction-heavy upfront subscription pricing, the platform scales monetization directly with identified corporate waste.

### Pricing Tiers & Monetization Structure
| Tier Name | Target Demographic | Pricing Mechanic | Annual Contract Value (ACV) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Growth Audit Plan** | Solo Founders & Freelancers | Free Core Engine Diagnostics | $0 (Self-Serve Growth Loop) |
| **Professional FinOps** | Growth Startups (<50 Employees) | 15% of Verified Annual Savings | Contingent on found leakage |
| **Enterprise Governance** | Scaled Technology Organizations | Custom Platform License + Audit Fee | $12,000 / year base fee |

---

## 2. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Strategy
- **Inbound Engineering Loop:** Developer tooling integrations (e.g., GitHub Action extension that alerts teams when their monthly infrastructure or seat tier commits hit redundant values).
- **Contingency-Led Sales Outbound:** Reaching out directly to CFOs and engineering directors with a baseline guarantee: *"If we don't save you at least $5,000 within 30 days, you pay us absolutely zero."*

## 3. Projected Run-Rate & Margin Parameters
- **Gross Margins:** ~92% (Low dynamic overhead; computation relies on clean deterministic static analytical checks via Next.js client-side execution nodes).
- **Target Retention (NDR):** 115% via proactive continuous automated optimization features (alerting teams as soon as team churn results in unutilized seat allocations).
